The comprehensive analytical pipeline is structured into five main parts: (1) Identification and validation of sex-specific phenotypic profiles, detailing the estimation of BMI–biomarker discrepancies, probabilistic profile discovery (UMAP projection and graph clustering), and robust cross-cohort replication; (2) Metabolic pathways, describing the identification of profile-associated metabolites and sex-specific pathway enrichment analyses; (3) Cardiovascular outcomes, comprising probability-weighted disease risk estimation, evaluation of added predictive value via nested models, and the clinical interpretation of profile shifts; (4) Longitudinal profile transitions, evaluating baseline-to-follow-up phenotypic trajectories (e.g., progressing and reversing transitions) and their impact on incident cardiovascular risk; and (5) Lifestyle-metabolite mediation, assessing how baseline lifestyle factors influence future profile probabilities through circulating metabolites using bootstrap-based mediation analysis.
